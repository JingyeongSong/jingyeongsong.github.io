Jingyeong Song

I am a sociologist who studies how social and institutional conditions shape migration, social integration, inequality, and vulnerability. My primary research focuses on post-displacement resettlement, social connectedness, and the health and well-being of refugees. My broader work extends to migration policy and public attitudes, social and institutional structures, and gender-based and interpersonal violence. Across this work, I aim to contribute to evidence-based policy that supports the long-term well-being of displaced and vulnerable populations.
